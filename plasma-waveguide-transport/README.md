# Plasma Waveguide Transport
## A Self-Contained Research & Engineering Package
**Version 1.0 — February 2026**

This repository contains a **numerically and physically corrected** investigation of magnetized plasma waveguides for guided energy and particle transport. The work explicitly avoids superluminal claims, refractive-index artifacts, and speculative spacetime manipulation. It focuses on **group-velocity-limited transport**, **ponderomotive channel formation**, and **wakefield acceleration**, all of which are experimentally grounded.

### What This Is
- A **plasma physics and engineering study**
- A **lab-testable concept**
- A **scaling-aware framework** for future space applications

### What This Is Not
- Not faster-than-light transport
- Not warp drive physics
- Not topology change in GR
- Not speculative propulsion claims
