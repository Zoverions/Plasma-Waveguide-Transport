# Background & Motivation

Across scales, efficient transport systems avoid pushing through resistance and instead **structure the medium**:

- Optical fibers guide photons
- Plasma channels guide charged particles
- Magnetic field lines guide whistler and Alfvén modes

In space, plasma is ubiquitous. The question addressed here is:

> Can magnetized plasma be structured into a **temporary waveguide** that enables efficient, guided energy and particle transport without onboard propellant?

This work answers that question **only within known plasma physics**.
