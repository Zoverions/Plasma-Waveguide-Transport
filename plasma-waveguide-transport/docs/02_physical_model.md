# Physical Model

### Plasma Parameters

- Electron density: $n_e$
- Magnetic field: $B$
- Electron temperature: $T_e$

### Fundamental Frequencies

$$
\omega_p = \sqrt{\frac{n_e e^2}{\epsilon_0 m_e}}
$$

$$
\omega_c = \frac{eB}{m_e}
$$

Where:
- $\omega_p$ = plasma frequency
- $\omega_c$ = electron cyclotron frequency
